# Freeman Eclectic Site — Interview Transcript

Every question asked during the vision interview on 2026-09-01, with Grant's answers. Recommended options
are marked. Later reconciliation questions (after BRAND.md surfaced) are included at the end.

## Round 1 — Roots

**Q1. What Freeman Eclectic actually makes?**
Small goods: cutting boards, board games, keychains, slate coasters, glassware, custom items. The business
has a large CNC machine and several kinds of lasers that engrave slate, glass, wood, metal, and acrylic.

**Q2. The one job the site has?**
Commission inquiry (recommended).

**Q3. Who is on the other side of the screen?**
Mostly small to medium businesses commissioning either a single larger-ticket item or a bulk order of
smaller-ticket items. Gift buyers are a secondary audience.

**Q4. What "Eclectic" means here?**
Mixed materials and mixed objects. From a slate coaster to a handcrafted wooden board game.

## Round 2 — Remaining roots

**Q5. Who is Freeman, and who runs the shop?**
Family/personal name, solo maker.

**Q6. What exists already?**
Logo or wordmark; product photography; social handle / domain.

**Q7. How much site?**
Multi-page, capability-led (recommended): Home, Work, Custom & Bulk, About, Contact.

**Q8. Keep the React Router + Tailwind stack, and how should the contact form work?**
Keep stack, mailto/DM only. No form.

## Round 3 — Brand

**Q9. Hand vs machine positioning?**

brass detail.

**Q12. Typography feel?**
Sturdy serif + clean sans (recommended).
